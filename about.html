About

A little background.

Theoretical physicist working on correlated phases of quantum matter.

I am Ke Wang, a theoretical physicist studying the correlated phases of quantum matter. My research area is condensed matter physics and ultracold atoms. Both systems exhibit intriguing few- and many-body physics. My research work is dedicated to discovering new physics and exploring mechanisms in these systems.

I am an Eberly Senior Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Rochester. Previously, I was a Kadanoff Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Chicago. I received my Ph.D. in Physics from the University of Massachusetts Amherst.
